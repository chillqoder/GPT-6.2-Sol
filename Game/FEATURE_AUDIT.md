# DEEPWAKE feature audit

The first playable candidate was checked using the fixed-step simulation and Chromium keyboard input. The improvement pass addressed the following findings.

| Feature or defect | Current state and evidence | Player benefit | Priority | Decision | Acceptance check | Final status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wall contact and shaft reach | **Broken** in the candidate. Steering away could hide the contacted wall. The two first rebounds needed more left-wall height. | Wall jumps respond to the intended direction. | P0 | Probe both sides and raise the second rebound wall in each shaft. | Rebound twice from safe ground in the reservoir and ascent. | **Working.** Implemented in `src/game.js` and `src/level.js`. Both keyboard tours cleared both shafts. |
| Reservoir docks and archive | **Broken** in the candidate. A 0.01-unit dock lip stopped ILO. The archive intersected a shutter cap. | The car has a clean exit and the optional shelf rewards the climb. | P0 | Align the two docks and shorten the cap. Assign the upper pocket to the reservoir. | Ride the car to the far deck. Reach the upper archive by wall jump and dash, read it, and return. | **Working.** Verified in both browser tours and the simulation. The HUD shows Crystal Reservoir on the shelf. |
| Checkpoints, cells, and gates | **Working** in the candidate. Each relay committed the socket and route together. | Retry restores a solvable world and keeps retained discoveries. | P0 | Verify atomic rollback, guarded closure, and restart. | Retry with changed cells, discoveries, powers, and hazards. Remove the foundry cell after latching the route. | **Working.** `src/game.js` passed the isolation checks and 15 repeat recovery/restart cycles. The full-archive browser tour retried at the ascent station and kept all six discoveries. |
| Lift and personal records | **Partial** in the candidate. Both routes completed, and the saved and loaded record objects differed in key order. | Results remain consistent across a reload and a new expedition. | P1 | Use one record shape and check the three relay IDs explicitly. | Verify boarding bounds, stopped clock, one result commit, replay, full-archive qualification, and reload. | **Working.** Verified in `src/game.js`, `src/records.js`, and Chromium. Record validation also passed with corrupt data and blocked storage. |
| Echo and cell feedback | **Partial** in the candidate. The tutorial described stun without prompting the dash. The key strip looked identical with a cell in hand. | Players can read vulnerability and carrying restrictions at a glance. | P1 | Prompt a dash near a stunned target. Dim unavailable input hints. | Pulse a machine, check stun and contact rules, then pick up a cell and exercise the blocked abilities. | **Working.** Implemented in `src/game.js`, `src/ui.js`, and `style.css`. Shield, pulse, carrying, socket priority, and single ownership passed simulation checks. |
| Touch controls and results | **Partial** in the candidate. The landscape result scroller was covered by a parent touch restriction. | The control layout works on narrow screens and tall panels can be read by touch. | P1 | Limit touch gesture capture to the canvas and movement buttons. | Move and jump together, cancel touch, open pause, and swipe the short landscape results. | **Working.** Verified with Chromium touch input at 393 × 852 and 812 × 375. A swipe scrolled the result panel by 182 CSS pixels. |
| Platform edges and lighting | **Partial** in the candidate. Coplanar platform faces flickered. The workshop portal was clipped and the dawn opening was hard to see. | Landings and the surface destination stay legible. | P2 | Separate platform faces, reframe shafts, and tune lights and the terrace. | Inspect the workshop, reservoir crossing, foundry latch, and terrace at gameplay scale. | **Working.** Visually checked the final Three.js desktop frames and portrait and landscape layouts. |
| Crushing presses | **Missing** by choice. | The foundry's active hazards stay focused on the sentry and vent timing. | Future | Use presses as animated scenery. Keep crush handling for sustained platform obstruction. | Reproduce a pinned upward lift with a ceiling fixture and verify one recovery. | Intentionally deferred as an authored hazard. The platform obstruction fixture passes. |

## Verification results

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Simulation | `npm test` passed 22 checks. The tests exercise jump height, coyote time, jump buffering, dash contacts, pulse obstruction and recharge, shielding, all three machine types, vent timing, cell ownership, moving platforms, shutdown, snapshots, records, and both complete routes. |
| Required browser route | Chromium at 1440 × 900, using the real keyboard handlers and rendered Three.js frames. Relay A, the crossing, Relay B, the counterweight latch, Relay C, the service lift, and the final terrace all passed. Active expedition time was **1:03.8**, with zero recoveries, discoveries, or disables. The same scripted input sequence passed in the simulation with zero shield or integrity hits. |
| Exploration browser route | All three archives and all three caches were collected. A deliberate ascent retry restored the committed discoveries. The route finished in **1:17.5** of active time with one recovery. Reload preserved both time records and the zero-recovery record. |
| Live frame loop and audio | Keyboard movement, pause, resume, lost focus, retry, and full restart passed in the standard page. A Web Audio analyser detected output after a gesture. Output fell below the test threshold while muted and paused. The visibility handler was exercised with a simulated hidden document. |
| Compatibility | A Chromium session denying WebGL, audio initialization, and local storage still accepted movement through the Canvas renderer. The desktop runs reported zero uncaught page errors. |

ILO's measured ordinary jumps reach about 1.22 units on a tap and 2.59 units on a hold. An unobstructed dash uses 20 fixed simulation steps, about 0.167 seconds. The playable route covers about 225 horizontal units from ILO's wake-up point to the terrace, with climbs and optional detours. Loose cells rest on static terrain and are shaped as interactables.

The route times above come from optimized scripted input. First-playthrough pacing against the brief's 8–12 minute target remains unmeasured. Touch testing used Chrome emulation. Audio verification used the generated signal and playback controls. Hardware performance and cross-browser behavior remain unmeasured.
